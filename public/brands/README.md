# Collaboration brand assets

The portfolio owner supplied this collaboration list on September 25, 2026.
The marks identify those collaborations, not product ownership or employer
affiliation. All marks remain the property of their respective owners.
The assets are self-hosted without recoloring or redrawing. Cards and bold
Google Sans captions follow the active theme. Each image is centered in the
same 120 by 40 pixel box inside a 128 by 48 pixel neutral backing, preserving
the original proportions while keeping dark/color marks readable in both
themes. The backing is white in light mode and muted gray-green in dark mode.
